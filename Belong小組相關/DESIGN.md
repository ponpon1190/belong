# Subtitle visual identity

## Style prompt

Clean, high-contrast social-video subtitles that remain readable over an unknown summer-drink reel. The visual treatment is deliberately restrained: bold white Traditional Chinese type with a semi-transparent black outline, gentle fades, and no additional graphics that could compete with the original footage.

## Colors

- `#FFFFFF` — subtitle text
- `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)` — readable outline
- `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.34)` — soft shadow

## Typography

- `Noto Sans TC` / `Source Han Sans TC`, 700 weight

## What NOT to Do

- Do not cover the centre of the video or the upper subject area.
- Do not use colour pills, gradients, or decorative text effects.
- Do not use abrupt subtitle cuts; use 0.3-second fades.
